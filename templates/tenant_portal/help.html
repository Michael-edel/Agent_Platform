Help & Support

Getting Started
  1. Get your portal key from your platform administrator
  2. Login at /tenant/login with your tenant ID and key
  3. Check Dashboard → Status should be OK
  4. Review your Subscription and Plan details
  5. Monitor Usage & Limits regularly
Understanding Status
OK Everything is working normally. No errors in the last 24 hours.
Degraded There are some errors in the last 24 hours. Check Dashboard for details.
Unknown Temporary issue accessing diagnostics. Try again later.
Plan Limits

If you exceed your plan limits, operations may be rejected with HTTP 429. To resolve:

  • Check your current usage on the Dashboard
  • Wait for the next billing period (limits reset monthly)
  • Contact support to upgrade your plan

Support Bundle

When contacting support, please attach your Support Bundle. It contains:

  • Subscription and plan details
  • Status and error counts (last 24h)
  • Usage limits and remaining
  • Recent events (metadata only, no sensitive data)

The bundle does not contain any passwords, tokens, or raw payloads.
